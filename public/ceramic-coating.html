What is Ceramic Coating?

Ceramic coating is a liquid polymer that chemically bonds with your vehicle's factory paint, creating a permanent or semi-permanent layer of protection. Unlike wax or sealants that sit on top of the paint, ceramic coating becomes part of your vehicle's surface.

The result is a hard, glass-like layer that protects against environmental damage while providing an incredible depth of gloss. Water, dirt, and contaminants bead up and roll off, making your vehicle easier to clean and maintain.

Our Coating Process

  • Thorough wash and decontamination
  • Clay bar treatment for smooth surface
  • Paint correction if needed
  • Panel-by-panel coating application
  • Curing time and final inspection

Protection Benefits

  • UV ray protection
  • Chemical and stain resistance
  • Bird dropping protection
  • Tree sap resistance
  • Easier cleaning and maintenance
  • Enhanced paint depth and clarity

Application Time:

Ceramic coating requires proper preparation and curing time. Plan for a full day for the complete process. Your vehicle will need to stay dry for 24-48 hours after application.
