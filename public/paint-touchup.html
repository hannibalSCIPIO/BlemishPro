Professional Paint Touch-Up at Your Location

Paint chips and minor paint damage are more than cosmetic issues. When bare metal is exposed, rust can start forming within days. Our professional paint touch-up service seals and protects your vehicle while restoring its appearance.

We use your vehicle's factory paint code to ensure a perfect color match. Our multi-step process includes proper surface preparation, primer application when needed, and clear coat protection for lasting results.

Our Touch-Up Process

  • Identify factory paint code
  • Clean and prep damaged areas
  • Apply rust inhibitor if needed
  • Carefully apply matched paint
  • Seal with clear coat protection

Paint Issues We Fix

  • Rock chips on hood and bumper
  • Door edge chips
  • Paint chips from road debris
  • Minor paint scratches
  • Touch-up after dent repair
  • Small rust spot treatment

Pro Tip:

Don't wait to fix paint chips. What starts as a tiny chip can become a rust spot that spreads under the paint, requiring much more expensive repairs later.
