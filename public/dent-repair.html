What is Paintless Dent Removal (PDR)?

Paintless Dent Removal is a specialized technique that removes dents from your vehicle's body panels without the need for body filler, sanding, or repainting. Using precision tools, we carefully massage the metal back to its original shape from behind the panel.

This method preserves your vehicle's original factory finish, maintains its resale value, and costs significantly less than traditional body shop repairs.

Why Choose PDR?

  • Preserves original factory paint
  • 50-70% cheaper than traditional repair
  • Completed in hours, not days
  • Environmentally friendly - no chemicals
  • Maintains vehicle resale value

Types of Dents We Repair

  • Door dings from parking lots
  • Hail damage repair
  • Minor collision dents
  • Shopping cart dents
  • Sports ball impacts
  • Creases and body line dents

Note:

PDR works best when the paint is intact. If your dent has cracked or chipped paint, we'll discuss the best repair options during your estimate.
