Why Headlights Get Cloudy

Modern headlight lenses are made of polycarbonate plastic, which is strong and impact-resistant but susceptible to UV damage. Over time, exposure to sunlight causes the plastic to oxidize, resulting in that familiar yellow, hazy appearance.

Cloudy headlights aren't just an eyesore—they're a safety hazard. Oxidized lenses can reduce your headlight output by up to 80%, significantly impacting your visibility at night and in bad weather conditions.

Our Restoration Process

  • Multi-stage wet sanding to remove oxidation
  • Professional compound polishing
  • UV-resistant sealant application
  • Final buff for crystal clarity

Signs You Need Restoration

  • Yellowed or discolored lenses
  • Hazy or foggy headlights
  • Oxidized plastic
  • Reduced night visibility
  • Failed state inspection
  • Faded appearance

Longevity Tip:

After restoration, park in the shade when possible and apply UV sealant annually to prevent re-oxidation and keep your headlights clear for years.
