Expert Bumper Repair at Your Location

Bumper damage is one of the most common vehicle repairs. Whether it's a parking lot scrape, a minor fender bender, or a curb impact, our mobile bumper repair service restores your bumper without the hassle of a body shop.

We specialize in repairing plastic bumper covers—the most common type on modern vehicles. Using professional techniques and materials, we repair damage and blend paint to match your vehicle's factory finish.

Why Mobile Bumper Repair?

  • No rental car needed
  • Repairs done while you work or relax
  • Factory-quality color matching
  • Fraction of body shop prices
  • Same-day service available

Bumper Damage We Repair

  • Scuffs and scrapes
  • Minor cracks and splits
  • Paint damage and chips
  • Parking lot bumps
  • Fender bender damage
  • Curb and pole impacts

Repair vs. Replace:

Many bumpers can be repaired rather than replaced, saving you hundreds or even thousands of dollars. We'll assess the damage and recommend the most cost-effective solution.
